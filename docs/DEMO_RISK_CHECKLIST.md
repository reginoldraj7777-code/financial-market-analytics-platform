# Interview Demo Risk Checklist

- Run `PREFLIGHT_CHECK_WINDOWS.bat` the day before and again 30 minutes before the interview.
- Keep the dashboard open before joining the call.
- Use browser zoom between 90% and 100%.
- Close personal email and unrelated tabs before screen sharing.
- Do not configure or show real credentials.
- Do not claim live Snowflake experience unless a live connection was genuinely tested.
- Clearly state that GTM and operational datasets are synthetic.
- Demonstrate Tabs 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, and 8 only unless asked.
- Keep a local PDF/screenshot backup of the main views.
- Stop after 7 minutes and invite questions.
